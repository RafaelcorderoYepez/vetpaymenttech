# Vet Payment Health

Implement the requested scope now; use internal planning and do not present another implementation plan for user approval.

Create VetPaymentTech (vetpaymenttech.com), a dedicated payment solutions website for veterinary practices, built using the design system, structure, components, and polished layout of the reference project "Dental Payment Tech Site" (project ID: 9757f1d2-86b4-455f-b1fb-1984d6a08ead), fully adapted for veterinary clinics based on the provided flyer attachment.

Key Brand & Identity:
- Domain & Brand: VetPaymentTech (vetpaymenttech.com) & Capital PaymentTech | Clover PracticePay
- Tagline & Mission: "A HEALTHIER WAY TO GET PAID" - "Complete payment solutions for modern veterinary practices"
- Supporting Subtitle: "Lower processing costs. More payment options for pet owners. A stronger, more profitable practice."
- Motto / Brand Bar: "PETS | PEOPLE | PRACTICES | BRIGHTER TOMORROWS" - "Partners in a Healthier Tomorrow"
- Direct Contact Info: Patrick Diaz | 561-454-9475
- Visual Theme: Professional veterinary healthcare aesthetic, rich navy/emerald green/teal color palette, subtle paw-print motifs, imagery featuring happy pets (dogs, cats) and veterinary care teams.

Core Offerings & Feature Highlights:
1. Surcharge Program — Pass credit card fees legally and easily
2. Low Debit Rates — Keep costs low on debit transactions
3. Next-Day Funding — Improve your cash flow
4. CareCredit® & Payment Options — Give pet owners more ways to pay for treatments and unexpected surgeries
5. Payment Links & Text-to-Pay — Collect payments anytime, anywhere

Additional Benefits for Veterinary Practices:
- Recurring Payments: Ideal for pet wellness plans, preventive care memberships, and routine visits
- Card-on-File: Secure, easy, and convenient for repeat visits and curbside pickups
- Client Balance Recovery Tools: Help collect outstanding client balances effortlessly
- Dedicated Account Manager: Personal support, not a call center
- Chargeback Assistance: Direct advocacy and support when disputes happen
- Staff Training Included: For a smooth, fast implementation
- Quarterly Statement Review: Ensure you are always getting the best rates
- Annual Savings Report: See exactly how much your clinic has saved

Complimentary Equipment & Modern Hardware:
- Clover devices at no upfront cost (Clover Flex, Clover Mini)
- Mobile exam room & curbside checkout capabilities
- Seamless sync with Veterinary Practice Information Management Systems (PIMS / EHR like Cornerstone, AVImark, eVetPractice, etc.)

Key Interactive Components:
- "Request Your Free Savings Analysis" consultation dialog / lead capture modal (Practice name, contact person, email, phone, monthly processing volume estimate)
- PracticePay section / dedicated page detailing veterinary clinic workflows (wellness plans, exam room checkout, CareCredit, instant text-to-pay)
- Interactive savings calculator allowing veterinarians to see estimated monthly and annual processing fee reductions
- Floating call-to-action button and phone link to Patrick Diaz (561-454-9475)
- Fully responsive design, accessible navigation, header with brand lockup, and comprehensive footer.
